I applied to 7 schools as a high school senior, all but one for a major in economics. For a number of reasons, I applied to major in Advertising at UT Austin, not the least because I thought it was in the business school. This choice was perhaps one of the best decisions in my life. 

## Major in Review: Advertising

My main regret about the ad program at UT was not taking ADV318J sooner. It all worked out in the end, but it should be a first semester class for Ad first-years. 

The first few courses of the advertising program are more theory based: 318J, 309R, 325, & 353, along with MKT 320F. The degree flowchart suggest spreading these course across the first 2 years, but I believe that they should be taken as soon as possible. In general, with the corrent prerequisites met, upper/lower division standing should have no bearing on studentt course selection. 

344K and 345J are geniuniely interesting and useful classes, but they should be taken second semester sophomore year. Without that foundation and project work, starting a career in advertising (paid internship) is difficult.
