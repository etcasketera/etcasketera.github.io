# The End of College Era

As I prepare to leave college and enter the working world, I want to reflect on the major aims that will drive this early career phase of life.

## Becoming a Subject Matter Expert (SME)

In a world with artificial intelligence, I believe that becoming a subject matter expert is more important than ever. Generalist skills and thinking can be rapidly emulated by artificial intelligence. Being able to push back against an LLM and recognize its mistakes will be crucial for creating differentiation.
